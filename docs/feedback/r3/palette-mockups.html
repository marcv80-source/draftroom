CC Boys Draft Room — palette mockups

Three ways to pull the app's colours out of the team logo. Every mock below uses the real board served by the running app at pick 1.01 on the blend source: real players, real draft values, real ADP, the real recommendation and its real bullets. Nothing here is sample data.

Each mock also previews ledger #13, the positional-rank chip (RB1, QB1) you asked for, so you can judge it in context while you look at the colour.

Nothing in the app has been changed. This is the approval step you asked for: pick one, or a mix, and that is when I touch the CSS.

A / Fairway

Keeps the dark draft-room ground, but moves it off generic black onto the logo's own navy. The gold accent retires in favour of the logo's mint. Smallest change of the three: layout, contrast and reading rhythm all stay where you have been testing them.

Read this one for: Closest to what is on screen now. Lowest risk for draft night.

B / Clubhouse

Commits to the logo's single most common colour, the deep fairway teal-green at 14% of the image, as the ground. Lime becomes the accent. This is the variant that most obviously reads as the same brand as the badge in the corner.

Read this one for: Most logo-faithful. A green ground is unusual for a data UI, so judge the row legibility hard.

C / Scorecard

The country-club scorecard read: cream ground, deep green chrome, wine for anything urgent. A real departure from a dark UI, and the only variant that looks like paper on a board rather than a terminal.

Read this one for: Boldest. Flagged risk: the draft is in person in a room whose lighting you do not control, and a cream field is the harshest of the three under a bright overhead or on a laptop at an angle.
